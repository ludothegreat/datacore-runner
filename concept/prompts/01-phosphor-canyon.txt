Concept art for a 3D endless runner video game, environment key art. Third-person camera behind and slightly above a lone runner figure made of glowing white-green wireframe light, sprinting down the centre of a long straight canyon between towering translucent holographic glass server monoliths of varied heights. Each monolith is a clear glass slab with bright edge lines and phosphor-green and teal-green glowing data glyphs, hex-dump-like columns and abstract symbols suspended inside it at different depths. The floor is glossy black, inlaid with glowing green circuit-board traces converging to a far vanishing point. Small warm orange accents: a few orange data packets streaking along the traces. Palette: phosphor green, teal-green, white highlights, deep black, small orange accents. Modern high-end rendering: volumetric haze, strong bloom, glossy ray-traced reflections, shallow depth of field, faint hologram scanline flicker on glowing surfaces. Retro-future 1990s cyberspace mood rebuilt with modern fidelity. Absolutely no blue, no purple, no yellow anywhere. No readable text, no letters, no logos, no UI or HUD, no watermark. Wide 16:9 cinematic landscape composition.

Concept art for a 3D endless runner video game, alternate zone key art called the thermal core. A single glowing orange light-rail track weaves between the cube stacks toward a bright molten-orange core on the horizon. A tiny runner figure of white light on the track for scale. Camera angled down from three-quarter high view. Palette: ember orange, crimson, muted red, warm white highlights, deep black. Modern high-end rendering: volumetric haze, strong bloom, glossy ray-traced reflections, shallow depth of field, faint hologram scanline flicker on glowing surfaces. Retro-future 1990s cyberspace mood rebuilt with modern fidelity. Absolutely no blue, no purple, no yellow anywhere. No readable text, no letters, no logos, no UI or HUD, no watermark.
